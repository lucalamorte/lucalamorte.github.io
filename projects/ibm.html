AI Innovation · IBM · client: Kraft Heinz · 2026 · present

Data model and AI agents

AI agents that Kraft Heinz's R&D and brand teams can ask, in plain language, about ingredients, nutrition and competitors.

Built with

  • Snowflake
  • dbt
  • Cortex AI Agents
  • Cortex Analyst
  • Semantic views

The goal

A question like “which competitors still use artificial dyes, and how do they sell?” needs two kinds of data at once: what each label says, and how much of each product sells. The goal is for the teams to ask it themselves and get the same number their dashboards would show.

How the data flows

Retail sales and product labelsboth already in Snowflake
Data modelfacts and dimensions in dbt that put both sources side by side
Semantic viewswhat each metric means, how tables join, synonyms and verified queries
Cortex AI Agentsturn the question into SQL with Cortex Analyst
R&D and brand teamsget the answer and the query behind it

Also part of the work

  • Deciding which AI use cases are worth building, and shipping the viable ones.
  • Taking agents from sandbox to production with the business owners and the data owners.

Client data, schemas and internal details are not shown.
