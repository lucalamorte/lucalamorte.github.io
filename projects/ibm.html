Data model and AI agents

The data model and semantic views behind Kraft Heinz's Cortex AI Agents. The project is in progress, so this write-up grows as it ships.

Built with

  • Snowflake
  • Cortex AI Agents
  • Cortex Analyst
  • Semantic views

The goal

An agent's numbers have to match the dashboards the teams already use. If the agent and a dashboard disagree, nobody trusts either of them.

How the data flows

Retail sales and product labelsalready in Snowflake: what sells, and what each label says
Data modelfacts and dimensions that put both sources side by side
Semantic viewsjoins, metric definitions, synonyms and verified queries
Cortex AI AgentsCortex Analyst writes the SQL from the semantic views
R&D and brand teamsthe people who ask the questions

Beyond the build

  • Use cases: AI use cases are evaluated for business value, and the viable ones are shipped.
  • Production: agents are taken from sandbox to production together with the business owners and the data owners.

Client data, schemas and internal details are not shown.
