Smart Manufacturing Operations Management

Real-time machine data and digital twins were rolled out in plants across Colombia, Argentina, El Salvador, Peru and the USA. Production data now sits next to SAP and ERP data, in one place to report from.

The problem

Each plant measured its lines in its own way, and machine data lived apart from the business data in SAP and the ERP. Comparing plants, or seeing in time how a line was really performing, meant reconciling numbers that were not defined the same.

How the data flows

Machines on the production lines10 plants, 5 countries
Real-time data collection and digital twinsa live model of each line
SAP and ERP data replicated into Azurejoined with production data
Standardized metricsone definition of each metric for every plant
Unified reporting layer in Power BIthe same numbers for every plant and team

What was done

  • Rollout: real-time machine data and digital twins went live in 10 plants across 5 countries, one plant at a time, with each team trained to use them.
  • Integration: SAP and ERP data was replicated into Azure, so production and business data could be read together.
  • Standardization: every plant agreed on one definition per metric, so Power BI shows the same numbers for all of them.
  • Leadership: the rollout was led end to end, as the first point of contact for business and technical teams on every project.

Built at Kimberly-Clark. Plant data and internal details are not shown.
