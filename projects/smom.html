The problem

Each plant measured its lines in its own way, and machine data lived apart from the business data in SAP and the ERP. Comparing plants, or seeing in time how a line was really performing, meant reconciling numbers that were not defined the same.

How the data flows

Machines on the production lines10 plants, 5 countries
Real-time data collection and digital twinsa live model of each line
SAP and ERP data replicated into Azurejoined with production data
Standardized metricsone definition of each metric for every plant
Unified reporting layer in Power BIthe same numbers for every plant and team

What was done

  • Rollout: the rollout of real-time machine data collection and digital twins was led across 10 plants in 5 countries, driving adoption end to end, plant by plant.
  • Integration: SAP and ERP data was replicated into Azure, so production and business data could be read together.
  • Standardization: metrics were standardized across plants, establishing a unified reporting layer for Power BI.
  • Impact: the program was tied to a $21.5M annual ROI.
