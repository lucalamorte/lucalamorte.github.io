Composable CDP

An in-house Customer Data Platform was built on Snowflake for North America Marketing, replacing a vendor platform. Customer data from many systems is unified into one profile per person and activated in the marketing tools from the warehouse itself.

Why composable

A packaged CDP copies customer data into its own store and charges for it. In a composable CDP the data warehouse is the single source of truth: profiles are modeled where the data already lives, and only the audiences that marketing needs are synced out to the activation platforms. The cost of the vendor goes away, and the data stays governed in one place.

What was built

  • Ingestion: ELT pipelines were architected to consolidate 12+ source systems from AWS S3 and APIs, with Python and SageMaker, orchestrated via Azure Data Factory and Control-M.
  • Modeling: Snowflake layers were modeled with daily incremental transformations across 35 dimension and fact tables, enabling cross-platform identity resolution and Customer 360 profiles for 65M users.
  • Delivery: Snowflake CI/CD was automated through Azure DevOps, so every change to the models goes through review and deploys the same way.
  • Trust: Monte Carlo was configured for data quality and anomaly detection, so a broken source is caught before it reaches an audience.
