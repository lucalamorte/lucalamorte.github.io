Composable CDP

An in-house Customer Data Platform was built on Snowflake by the North America Marketing data & analytics team (USA and Canada), replacing a vendor platform. Customer data from a dozen systems ends up as one profile per person, and marketing uses it straight from the warehouse.

Why composable

A packaged CDP copies customer data into its own store and charges for it. In a composable CDP the data warehouse is the single source of truth: profiles are modeled where the data already lives, and only the audiences that marketing needs are synced out to the activation platforms. The vendor bill goes away, and the data stays in one governed place.

How the data flows

Source systemsfiles in AWS S3 and APIs
ELT pipelines in Python and SageMakerorchestrated by Azure Data Factory and Control-M
Snowflake layers35 dimension and fact tables with daily incremental transformations
Identity resolution and Customer 360the same person matched across platforms
Activation through Hightouchto the marketing platforms

One person, end to end

A mother buys Huggies at a retailer, downloads a coupon on her phone and signs up for the newsletter with her email. Three systems see three strangers. This is how they become one profile and one audience. Table names are illustrative.

Three raw recordsa retailer purchase file in S3, a coupon app event, a newsletter signup
stg_purchases · stg_coupon_events · stg_signupscleaned and typed; emails and phones normalized and hashed
int_identity_graphthe hashed email and the device ID are matched, so the three records get one consumer_id
dim_consumer · fct_consumer_eventsone row per person; every purchase, download and signup as an event with its date and brand
Audience: Huggies buyers, last 30 days, opted ina query over the two tables, synced to the ad platforms every day

Delivery and data quality

  • Delivery: every change to the models goes through review and deploys the same way, with CI/CD in Azure DevOps.
  • Trust: Monte Carlo was configured for data quality and anomaly detection, so a broken source is caught before it reaches an audience.
